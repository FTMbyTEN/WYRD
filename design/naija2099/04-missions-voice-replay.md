# NAIJA 2099 — Part 4: Missions, Voice, Replayability

## 1. Twenty-five branching missions

Each mission: objective · at least three approaches · consequences · context · NPCs · the larger conflict it feeds. Combat is never the only way.

**Larger conflicts (arcs):** A — *Who owns the island?* (land & flooding) · B — *Who runs the city: WYRD, the State or the people?* · C — *The hustle and its price* (economy & corruption) · D — *Memory and culture*.

1. **Tomato Emergency** (Balogun) — Get 40 baskets of tomatoes to Balogun before the Eid rush after a northern supply truck stalls. Approaches: drive to Mile 12 and haggle; organise danfos via the union; pay a drone courier (expensive). Consequences: prices fall (traders love you) or you profit by holding stock (traders remember). NPC: Iya Rofiat, tomato wholesaler. Arc C.
2. **Ojuelegba Gridlock** — A broken traffic light jams the junction. Approaches: report to WYRD and redirect traffic by hand; hack the light; pay area boys to direct cars. Consequences: WYRD trust, Police/LASTMA reaction, area boys' expectation of future pay. NPC: Officer Ngozi (LASTMA). Arc B.
3. **The Floating School Run** (Makoko) — Deliver exam papers to the floating school before WAEC starts. Approaches: canoe through the creeks; borrow a speedboat from smugglers; get WYRD to hold a ferry. Consequences: smugglers want a favour; the headteacher becomes an ally. NPC: Mr Ayọ̀, teacher. Arc A.
4. **Owambe Under the Rain** (VI) — The wedding canopy collapses before the couple's entrance. Approaches: borrow canopies from a rival planner; move the party indoors; turn it into a rain dance with the DJ. Consequences: society reputation; rival's grudge or alliance. NPCs: the two mothers-in-law. Arc C.
5. **Lost Masters** (Surulere) — A dead highlife musician's master tapes are being auctioned abroad. Approaches: outbid with crowdfunding; prove ownership through ORÍKÌ archives; negotiate with the seller's conscience. Consequences: cultural vitality, a family's royalties. NPC: Baba Tunde, the musician's son. Arc D.
6. **Sea Wall Crack** (Eko Atlantic) — A crack threatens a storm-surge breach. Approaches: alert Atlantic Rise; leak it to journalists; patch it with the Waterfront Alliance's divers. Consequences: who gets credit, which coast floods if it fails. NPC: Engr. Hauwa, sea-wall engineer. Arc A.
7. **The Prophet's Miracle Water** (Mushin) — A pastor sells "blessed" water that is contaminated. Approaches: test the water and inform the Faith Coalition; expose him online; confront him privately to make him stop and refund. Consequences: congregation's trust (they may resent you), health of the district. NPC: Sister Grace, a church member. Arc C.
8. **Union Election** (Obalende) — Two candidates for motor-park chairman. Approaches: campaign for the reformer; broker a deal; expose vote-buying. Consequences: fares, levies and violence levels in the park. NPCs: Alhaji Bello, Mama Kemi (reformer). Arc C.
9. **Phone of the Minister** (Ikeja) — A minister's lost phone holds evidence of a land deal. Approaches: return it (reward and a favour); leak it; trade it to Atlantic Rise. Consequences: Arc A shifts sharply; State Govt and Youth Assembly react. NPC: the minister's driver, who found it. Arc A.
10. **Generator Wars** (Surulere street) — Neighbours fight over a shared generator during a blackout. Approaches: fix the grid connection; set up a solar co-op; mediate a schedule. Consequences: energy access, neighbourly relations. NPCs: Mr Okafor & Mrs Lawal. Arc C.
11. **The Eyo Procession** (Lagos Island) — Keep the Eyo route clear and safe. Approaches: work with the palace guards; coordinate WYRD traffic diversions; mobilise local youth. Consequences: cultural vitality, palace favour; disrespecting rules angers the community. NPC: the Ìdèjọ chief's aide. Arc D.
12. **Container 4B** (Apapa) — A container you're clearing holds medical supplies stolen from a hospital. Approaches: return to hospital; blow the whistle on customs; sell back to the thieves. Consequences: Ports Syndicate vs. hospitals, Customs reputation. NPC: Dr Bello. Arc C.
13. **Startup Buyout** (Yaba) — A foreign giant offers to buy your friend's app that runs Makoko's water taxis. Approaches: help her refuse and find local investors; negotiate protections; accept and share the money. Consequences: Yaba Collective, Waterfront, your friendship. NPC: Chidinma. Arc B.
14. **The Kidnapped Goat** (Festac) — A family's prize ram for Sallah is stolen. Approaches: track it via market gossip; trade for it; set a trap with the vigilantes. Consequences: comedy, Festac community trust. NPC: Malam Sani & his grandson. Arc D (comic).
15. **Flood Night** (Lekki) — Flash flood traps commuters on the expressway. Approaches: run boats; open WYRD's flood gates (flooding another area); organise volunteers to clear drains. Consequences: lives, displacement, which district blames you. NPC: Tunde, okada rider. Arc A.
16. **The Leak** (Ikoyi) — A source gives you SENTINEL logs proving estates profile visitors. Approaches: publish; take it to WYRD; use it to blackmail estates (questionable). Consequences: Ikoyi elite, Youth Assembly, WYRD's trust. NPC: a nervous gate-guard, Mustapha. Arc B.
17. **Pepper Soup Diplomacy** (Mushin) — Two rival recycling crews are about to fight. Approaches: host a peace meal at a buka; prove who's dumping; split the territory. Consequences: crime and pollution levels. NPC: Mama Ireti, buka owner. Arc C.
18. **Ghost Bus** (Third Mainland Bridge) — Passengers report a driverless danfo at night. Approaches: investigate (it's a hacked autonomous bus); hack it back; report to WYRD. Consequences: reveals AREA network's corruption. NPC: night conductor Sule. Arc B.
19. **Grandma's Land** (Ebute-Metta) — Your family's land title is contested. Approaches: archive research with ORÍKÌ; court; negotiate with the claimant family. Consequences: family relationships, displacement variable. NPC: Grandma Adunni. Arc A.
20. **Match Day** (National Stadium) — Prevent a riot after a disputed penalty. Approaches: get the VAR footage released; calm fans through a famous ex-player; slow the crowd with WYRD transit. Consequences: Surulere crime/vitality. NPC: Coach Emeka. Arc D.
21. **Japa or Stay** (Airport) — Your sibling has a visa interview; the family needs money. Approaches: fund it; persuade them to stay with a job; help them find a scholarship. Consequences: family arc, a future contact abroad. NPC: your sibling. Arc C.
22. **The Drone Strike** (Ikeja drone port) — Delivery-drone pilots strike over pay. Approaches: support the strike; cross the picket; negotiate. Consequences: deliveries city-wide, labour reputation. NPC: Ifeoma, union rep. Arc C.
23. **Recovered Voices** (Badagry Road) — Restore recordings of descendants' stories for a heritage centre. Approaches: travel to elders; crowdsource; negotiate with a museum abroad. Consequences: cultural vitality; deep, respectful storyline. NPC: Mrs Hunpatin. Arc D.
24. **Blackout Bank Run** (Broad Street) — A cyber-attack freezes bank systems; crowds panic. Approaches: help ÀJỌ provide cash; track the attackers; keep peace at branches. Consequences: Trust, Yaba Collective, banks. NPC: Mr Coker, branch manager. Arc B.
25. **The Gate Decision** (city-wide finale) — A once-in-a-century storm: WYRD can open gates to save Eko Atlantic or Makoko, not both fully. WYRD asks you. Approaches: choose one; find a third way using everything you've built (boats, unions, drones, faith halls as shelters); give the decision to a citizens' assembly. Consequences: the city's final shape and WYRD's future. Arcs A + B.

## 3. Replayability: authored story + AI-generated side events

**Always authored (never generated):** main arcs A–D and their 25 key missions; named major characters' personalities and histories; WYRD's hidden history; endings; anything involving real tragedies, religion's sacred core, or real public figures.

**AI may generate:**
- Side events from templates: a passenger with a story, a market price shock, a lost child at a festival, a stall dispute, a rescue on the lagoon.
- Gossip, radio talk, WYRD bulletins and diary lines (WYRD already writes its real diary in the WYRD app — the game uses the same voice).
- Minor NPC names, small talk and banter within the style guide.
- New delivery/danfo/rescue jobs placed on real map locations.

**Continuity:** A world-state ledger (district variables, faction standings, relationship memories, past events) is passed to every generator. Generated content can only reference people and facts in the ledger or create minor new ones. Major characters can't be killed, recast or contradicted by generated events.

**Cultural quality checks:** Generators are prompted with Part 3 §4; outputs pass an automated check (blocked stereotypes, slurs, real-person references, sacred-practice misuse) and a sampling human review before new templates ship. Players can flag an event; flagged templates are paused.

**Different versions of the city:** The nine district variables, ten faction balances, the finale decision and your businesses produce visibly different Lagoses: a flood-safe island with community land trusts and murals, or a gleaming Eko Atlantic beside a displaced waterfront, or something in between. New Game+ keeps the city's state and changes your background.
