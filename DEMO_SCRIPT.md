# WYRD: demo video script (under 2 minutes)

Record in the **Flutter client**: https://wryd00.serverpod.space/flutter

## Before you record

1. Sign in, switch to **dark mode**, set the browser to 100% zoom and close the other tabs.
2. **About 10 minutes before:** in Dialogue Link, ask
   *"Make me a game: Danfo Dash, a racer dodging Lagos traffic"*.
   When its tile on the Games tab says **PLAY**, open it once to check it works.
3. Tap **+ NEW CHAT** so the conversation starts clean.
4. Do one full practice run. Cut any waiting out in editing.

## The script

**0:00–0:12 · Home screen.** The brain is moving; point at "Last thought".
> "This is WYRD — an AI that keeps thinking when nobody's talking to it. That's its last thought,
> from a minute ago."

**0:12–0:30 · Dialogue Link.** Type: *Draw me a Lagos sunset over the lagoon*. The picture appears.
> "It answers with what you need — here, a picture it drew itself."

**0:30–0:45 · Memory.** Type: *Remember my exam is on Friday.* → **+ NEW CHAT** →
*What do you remember about me?*
> "One memory, shared by every chat and everything it does."

**0:45–1:05 · Games.** Apps → Games → the **Danfo Dash** tile → **WATCH WYRD PLAY**.
WYRD drives for a few seconds, then tap to take over.
> "Ask it for a game and it programs one into your Games tab — I asked for this ten minutes ago.
> And it plays its own games. Tap, and I take the wheel."

**1:05–1:25 · The WYRD button.** Still on Games, tap WYRD's face in the corner.
Type: *Where are we?* → then: *Put my games first.* The cards move.
> "Call it from any screen — it reads the page you're on, and it can change the app for you."

**1:25–1:35 · Chess.** Open Chess, make one move; WYRD answers.
> "It plays you at chess too — full strength."

**1:35–1:50 · Its mind.** Home → **ENTER 3D**; BRAIN_3D springs in.
> "Its mind, live from the server: what it knows, what it's digesting, what it learned today."

**1:50–2:00 · The code.** Show the GitHub README: github.com/FTMbyTEN/wyrd_serverpod
> "A Flutter client and one Serverpod backend — endpoints, database tables, and future calls that
> keep WYRD alive between conversations. Live now at wryd00.serverpod.space."
